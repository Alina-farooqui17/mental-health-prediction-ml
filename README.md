# 🧠 Solviera — AI-Powered Mental Health Prediction System

## 📌 Project Overview

**Solviera** is an AI-powered Mental Health Prediction System designed to analyze various behavioral, lifestyle, and personal factors to identify potential patterns associated with mental health concerns.

The project uses **Machine Learning** to transform raw data into meaningful insights and generate predictive assessments based on patterns learned from historical data. Solviera follows a complete end-to-end Machine Learning workflow, beginning with data collection and preprocessing, followed by exploratory data analysis, feature transformation, model training, evaluation, and finally the integration of the trained model into an interactive application.

Mental health is a complex and essential aspect of overall well-being. Factors such as lifestyle, personal circumstances, behavior, and other demographic or environmental variables may influence an individual's mental well-being. Through data-driven analysis, Solviera explores these relationships and demonstrates how Artificial Intelligence can be used to support early awareness and understanding of potential mental health risks.

The system accepts relevant user inputs through an interactive interface, processes the information using the trained Machine Learning pipeline, and generates a prediction based on patterns identified within the training data.

> ⚠️ **Disclaimer:** Solviera is developed for educational and research purposes only. It is not a medical diagnostic tool and does not provide medical or psychological advice. Its predictions should not replace consultation with qualified mental health professionals.

## 🎯 Vision

The vision behind **Solviera** is to demonstrate how Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning can be used responsibly to transform complex data into meaningful insights that may support mental health awareness and early intervention.
